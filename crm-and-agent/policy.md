# Kestrel Analytics – Sales Qualification Policy

Kestrel Analytics is a fictional company. This policy is an example for Felspar.

## Purpose

This policy decides which companies our account executives contact first, so every research agent and every CRM workflow at Kestrel qualifies a company the same way.

Use this policy when a company is added to the CRM, or when an agent researches a company before outreach.

Do not use it for existing customers: renewals and expansions follow the account management playbook.

The head of sales operations owns this policy.

## Who is qualified

A company is qualified for sales when it has at least 500 employees and an AI initiative is in progress.

A company with fewer than 500 employees is not qualified, whatever its AI plans.

A company whose AI work is only planned, or is some other kind of AI activity, is not qualified, even when it is large.

## Employee count

Count the company's employees as its own website, its LinkedIn page or the CRM record states them.

## AI initiative

What is the state of the company's AI work?

In progress: the company is building, piloting or running an AI project now. For example, "we launched our AI assistant to all customers" or "our pilot of AI-assisted claims review is live in two regions".

Planned: the company says it will start an AI project in a future quarter, or is hiring to start one. A plan is not progress. For example, "we plan to pilot generative AI next year" or "we are hiring our first machine learning engineer".

Other AI activity: the company mentions AI in some other way, such as reselling another vendor's AI features or writing about AI trends. For example, "read our guide to AI trends in logistics".

## Evidence

Use evidence from the last 90 days.

## Next steps

Qualified: create an account executive task in the CRM and start outreach within two business days.

Not qualified: add the company to the newsletter nurture and do not contact it directly.

## When the evidence does not decide

If the employee count or the state of the AI work is missing, research it once more. If it is still missing, or two sources give employee counts on both sides of the threshold, sales operations reviews the company before anyone contacts it.

## Companies

We identify each company by its web domain, such as kestrel.example.
